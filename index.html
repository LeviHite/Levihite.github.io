A Little About me

Where I am from 

I was born and raised in Elkhart, IN, and attended Bible Baptist Church in Wakarusa, IN. I am an Eagle Scout, the highest rank in Boy Scouts. I competed at the state level in swimming, and my best strokes were butterfly and freestyle.

What I am studying 

I currently attend Bob Jones University, and I am studying Business Finance with a minor in Global Ministries. I decided to go into finance after talking with my uncle about what he does.

Where I work

After a long interview process and prior work experience, I started my employment at Apple in August 2025 in a seasonal role. Since then, I have been offered and accepted a permanent position.

My Journey

  • 2017

    My first steps into robotics

    Growing up, I loved building with LEGOs and was always making something new. After hearing about a local robotics league that used LEGOs to create the robots, I signed up right away. Our team advanced all the way to the state competition, where we placed third in presentation.

  • 2021

    Achieving the Highest Rank

    Joining Boy Scouts when I turned 11 set me on a path to achieve the highest rank, Eagle Scout. This is one of the hardest achievements to earn. It took me five years to work my way up to Eagle. For my Eagle project, I renovated a playground at a local daycare. I raised 5,000 dollars and organized about five workdays to complete it.

  • 2022

    Becoming a Videographer

    For my first time living away from home, I moved to Minnesota and started an internship at Olstad Media, where I became a videographer. I also served as the lead social media manager, creating over 1,500 reels in just six months. The content I produced quickly grew the social media accounts and became very popular, reaching over 1 million views.

  • 2023

    Meeting the Love of My Life

    Our story began at The Wilds camp, where we quickly became best friends. After a while, we had the conversation about whether we should date, and before we knew it, we were engaged. Our special day is May 16, 2026.
